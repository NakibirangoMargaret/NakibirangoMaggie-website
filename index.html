CLEAN WATER AND SANITATION

"Pure Water, Pure Health"

Access to clean water and sanitation ia a basic huan right. However ,millions of people around the world still struggle to get safe drinking water and proper hygiene facilities. This project focuses on the importance of clean water and sanitation for healthy living and sustainable deevelopment.

objectives

To raise awareness about the importance of clean water and sanitation. To promote safe water practices in communities.To encourage investments in sanitation infrastructure. To reduce water borne diseases through education and access.

benefeciaries

The primary benefeciaries of this project include;

Rural communities lacking access to clean water. Schools and institions without proper sanitation facilities Women and children who are most affected by water scarcity.Health centers in need of hygiene conditions

Expected outcomes

Improved access to clean and safe drinking water. Reduction in waterborne diseases in targeted areas. Increased awareness about hygiene and sanitation practices. Empowered communities capable of maintaining water systems.
