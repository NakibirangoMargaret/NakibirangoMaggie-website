    Clean Water and Sanitation

    Vision

    To create a healthy and sustainablecommunitywhere every one has access to clean and safe water aswell as proper sanitation facilities.

    Mission

    To improve the quality of life through promoting clean water access ,sanitation ,education and hygiene practices using community paticipation and suitable solutions.

    Our goals

    • To provide clean and safe water sources to rural and urban communities.
    • To promote proper sanotation and hygiene practices among households and schools through sensitization.
    • To reduce water borne disease through awareness and infrastructural development like diging wells and boreholes.
    • To empower communities to maintain and manage their water and sanitation systems.
